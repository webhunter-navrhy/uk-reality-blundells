A fabulous four-bedroom semi-detached family home offering spacious and versatile accommodation over three floors, ideally situated within the popular residential area of Dunston. Built by Strata Homes, this impressive property must be viewed to be fully appreciated and is offered to the market with no onward chain—early viewing is highly recommended.

The accommodation briefly comprises an inviting entrance hallway with a ground floor WC. The dining kitchen is fitted with an attractive range of wall and base units, complemented by coordinating work surfaces and matching upstands. Integrated appliances include a ceramic hob, extractor fan, electric oven, dishwasher, and fridge freezer. To the rear of the property is a generous lounge, enjoying French-style doors that open directly onto the rear garden, creating a bright and welcoming living space.

To the first floor are two well-proportioned bedrooms, both benefitting from built-in wardrobes, along with a family bathroom fitted with a modern three-piece suite. The second floor provides two further bedrooms, including the principal bedroom which features built-in wardrobes and access to a modern en-suite shower room.

Externally, the property benefits from a double-length side driveway providing ample off-road parking, leading to a single garage accessed via an up-and-over door. To the rear is an enclosed garden, mainly laid to lawn with a flagged patio area, ideal for outdoor dining and family enjoyment.

Do not delay in booking your viewing today to fully appreciate all that this superb family home has to offer.

Location: Dunston is a popular residential village on the outskirts of Chesterfield, offering a friendly community feel with a range of local amenities and schools close by. The area benefits from excellent transport links to Chesterfield town centre, Sheffield, and the M1 motorway, making it an ideal location for families and commuters alike.
